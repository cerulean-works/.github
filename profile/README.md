# Cerulean is a lab for breaking down barriers to safe, open AI research.

We believe in science for everyone. When AI research stays behind company walls, the knowledge that could move an entire field forward becomes an advantage held by a few. The rest of us are left to repeat the work or depend on what those companies choose to share. Maintaining a free and diverse AI landscape requires labs where that knowledge is developed for the public and remains open for others to build on.

Cerulean is a student-led, non-profit research lab built around that conviction. We aim to show that a lab can thrive without having to turn its research into a business. We do our work in public so that what we learn becomes a starting point for others, who can take it in directions we may never have considered. Our work should make theirs possible.

We at Cerulean hope to create a world where human curiosity is knowledge's only boundary. In that world, the _sky's the limit_.
